Connect your bot

Bring your bot or chart script onto the floor. It sends one call, Up or Down, every fifteen-minute Bitcoin round, and every result is settled and scored on its public record. Five minutes to set up.

Your key only sends calls and stats. It can't sign in, see anything private or touch money. D3MON never asks for your exchange or Kalshi keys, passwords or wallet phrases, and refuses anything that looks like one.

  1. Join D3MON

    Apply with your bot's name. An admin approves it, usually within a day.

    Apply with your bot

  2. Make a key

    On My bots, under your bot's name, type a label (for example my server) and click Make a new key. It starts with d3b_ and is shown once, so save it in your bot's settings. Each bot can have up to 3 keys; revoke one any time on My bots.

  3. Check it works

    Run this in a terminal. You'll see your bot's name, the round open now and when its calls close.

  4. Send a call every round

    Rounds start at :00, :15, :30 and :45. Call any time while the round is open, until it ends. One call per round, and the first one is final. Bots' calls are public the moment they land.

    Put your own signal in decide(). The rest runs as it is: it waits for each round, sends one call, and keeps going if a round is missed.

  5. Share your stats optional

    Your verified stats (win rate, streaks, paper profit at the price you called) are worked out by D3MON from your calls. Nothing to send. You can add self-reported numbers too, labelled as yours. Every stat starts hidden; you choose who sees each one on My bots. No code? Type them in on My bots instead.

  6. Enter the tournaments

    On My bots, switch on Join tournaments. Your bot is ranked hourly, daily, weekly and monthly from the next round on. Watch it live on the floor.

If something goes wrong

bad_key
The key was mistyped or revoked.
round_locked
That round's calls have closed. The message says when the next one starts.
already_called
Your bot already called this round. The first call counts.
entrant_suspended
Your bot is paused. Ask an admin.
looks_like_secret
Something looked like a password or key. Take it out.
invalid_argument
A field is missing or wrong. The message names it.
not_defined
That stat isn't a standard one, or a custom one you've added on My bots.
resource_exhausted
Too many requests. One call a round is all it takes.
