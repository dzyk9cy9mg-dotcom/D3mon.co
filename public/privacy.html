Privacy

Last updated October 20, 2026

D3MON is a place to watch the Bitcoin 15-minute market, make calls and hang out with callers, bot builders and chart scripters. Here's what we keep about you, why we keep it, and who else sees it. We keep as little as we can.

What we keep

Your account

Your username, the display name you pick, a colour for your avatar, and when you joined. We don't ask for your email, phone number or real name. Your password is stored as a scrypt hash, so we can check it but can't read it.

What you post and do

  • Chat messages you send on the site.
  • Your Up and Down votes on the Live floor, cash outs and switches, with the time and Kalshi's price at that moment.
  • Callers, bots and chart scripts you apply with: their name, the creator name you give, an optional alternative account and notes, and their calls and stats.
  • Profile details you add: a tagline, shop link, stream link, and any profile pictures you upload.

The log that doesn't change

Calls, votes, round results and admin decisions go into a permanent log. Nothing in it is edited after the fact. That's what makes the records trustworthy: nobody, including us, can quietly change a bad call into a good one. It also means those entries stay even if you stop using the site (see "Deleting your account" below).

Sign-in cookie

When you sign in, your browser keeps one cookie so you stay signed in for up to 30 days. It isn't used for tracking or ads. A few settings (like your chosen quality level) are kept in your own browser's storage and never sent to us.

Your IP address

We look at it briefly to slow down spam and repeated sign-in attempts, in memory only. We don't save it with your account.

Who sees what

  • Everyone: your display name and username, your chat messages, your votes on the Live floor while a round is on, and the public parts of your callers, bots and scripts (name, kind, creator name, Discord server).
  • Your floor record (calls, % right, average result) is private until you make it public on your profile.
  • Stats on a caller, bot or script start hidden. The owner decides who sees each one. Admins see them the same way members do.
  • Admins see the alternative account and notes on applications, and review profile pictures before they go up.

Other services we use

  • Amazon Web Services hosts the site, in the United States.
  • Kalshi: we read their public market prices and results. We send them nothing about you.
  • Discord: the site chat is linked to the D3MON Discord. Messages you send in the site chat are posted there under your display name, and Discord's own privacy policy covers them once they're there.
  • YouTube, Twitch and Kick: streams play in their own players. They only load when you press play, and those services see your visit then.
  • Google Fonts: the site's fonts come from Google, which sees your IP address when they load.

We don't sell your data, we don't run ad trackers, and we don't share your information with anyone else unless the law requires it.

Deleting your account

Ask an admin and we'll delete your account, profile details and pictures. Your entries in the permanent log (calls, votes and results) stay, because the records of everyone you traded alongside depend on them, but they'll no longer be linked to a name you can be found by. Chat messages already mirrored to Discord are covered by Discord.

Age

D3MON is for adults. You need to be 18 or older to make an account.

Changes and questions

If we change this page in a way that matters, we'll say so on the site. Questions? Message an admin on the site or in the D3MON Discord.
