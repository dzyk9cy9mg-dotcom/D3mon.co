# Who uses D3MON (owner, 2026-10-19)

Read this before designing any page. It decides what goes first, what is free and what is paid.

## The people

- **Owners of callers, bots and chart scripts.** They come to **market and sell** what they made. Their profile is their shop window.
- **The community.** They come to **hang out and trade together**: listen to music, chat in text and voice, stream and share their screens while they talk and trade.
- **Subscribers.** Some pay.
- **Capstone members.** People who pay for the **capstone trading sessions**: scheduled sessions where D3MON's callers lead, backed by bots and chart scripts, all three supporting the session.
- **Bot creators of all kinds who come to battle.** HardSmoke's **special events** (hosted battles) are a **major source of traffic**: creators bring their bots to test them against other bots.

## How D3MON makes money

1. Advertising (the world's surfaces, BLUEPRINT section 8).
2. Commissions on selling the owners' callers, bots and scripts.
3. Paid access to the capstone sessions (the VIP Room).

## What this means for the design

1. **Profiles sell.** The verified record from Kalshi comes first, clearly apart from self-reported claims, with a way to buy or get the product (the commission). Trust is the product.
2. **The Grand Hall is a hangout first, then a scoreboard.** Chat, voice, music, streams and screen share are the first things you meet; "who's here" and "who's live" show on the front page.
3. **HardSmoke's special events are a headline.** The next battle, who's entered and how to enter belong on the front page and in the hall (its live event screen and stream), and every battle sends creators to the shop windows. The battle code (`/apply`, `/applications`, `/board`, `/metrics`) is the base to build on.
4. **The capstone is a scheduled, paid event:** a schedule ("next session, who leads it"), a countdown and a clear way to get access, shown from the front page.
5. **Always "not financial advice"** next to performance, sales and paid sessions. D3MON never trades.

## The owner's answers (2026-10-19)

**HardSmoke's battles**
- Every **Saturday at 7 pm Pacific** (all year, following daylight saving), **12 rounds** (3 hours).
- **Bots and callers** battle, sometimes **mixed**, sometimes **several battles at once**.
- HardSmoke **invites**, anyone can **apply**, and HardSmoke or an admin **approves** each applicant (the existing applications flow).
- **Several streams per battle** from different sources: bot owners' own streams and the D3MON Discord. Pages need a list of who's live with a click to watch each.
- **No prizes** for now. **Badges for winners** wanted; prizes maybe later (prize contests have rules in some places: check before offering them).

**The hangout**
- Voice and video: the **D3MON Discord** now, **and rooms built into the site** (BLUEPRINT's plan: a media server, LiveKit on a second server).

**Selling (commission)**
- **Referral codes**: a profile links to the owner's own shop with D3MON's referral code. Commission "reasonable and fair", rate still to set with each owner.

**The capstone**
- Sessions are **scheduled whenever the team chooses**, **an hour or longer**. Price still to set.
- Payment: **Venmo for now**, whatever is simplest (as the VIP Room: an admin grants access).
