# D3MON World: blueprint

**Status: draft for the owner's approval (updated 2026-10-17: the walkable spiral ramp is replaced by the triple helix of entrants; see section 4).** This is the one design that every build step follows. It turns the [vision](VISION.md), the [inspiration](INSPIRATION.md) and the [decisions](QUESTIONS.md) into a single world.

Diagrams: [`blueprint/map-top.svg`](blueprint/map-top.svg) (seen from above) and [`blueprint/section-side.svg`](blueprint/section-side.svg) (cut through the side).

## 1. The idea in one line

The D3MON triangle is a **pyramid of light in deep space**. Inside it, a **triangular spiral** climbs from the **Arena** on the floor to the **Trade Floor** at the peak, passing the Callers, Bots and Chart Scripts on every turn.

## 2. The look

- **Space and floor:** the original background: near-black `#05080C`, the faint blue flower-of-life pattern, now a floor of light stretching to the horizon, and the soft blue glow above.
- **Colours with meaning:** amber `#FFB547` Callers, blue `#4DA3FF` Bots, violet `#B98CFF` Chart Scripts, green `#2EE59D` up, red `#FF4D6A` down. White-blue where the three meet.
- **Light, not stuff:** edges, rails and walls are drawn as **lines of light and dark glass**. Solid, heavy objects are kept rare. That's the look, and it's what keeps it fast.
- **Engraved line art** (from `inspiration/08`): our emblems are drawn as intricate engraved line art in the three colours on black: the triangle, the pyramid, the astronaut, and the Callers, Bots and Scripts marks. It's used on the loading screen, profile frames, billboard frames and the 2D version, and it glows on the glass in 3D.
- **Type:** Orbitron for big titles, Rajdhani for text, Share Tech Mono for HUD labels. Huge two-tone headlines (from `inspiration/06`): "I am **NeuroBot 9.**"
- **Motion:** slow, weighted, with momentum; nothing snaps. Particles of light carry every change from one place to the next (from `inspiration/07`).
- **Sound:** off until the visitor turns it on. An eerie, echoing score with no water-like sounds. Sound cues for the lock and reveal.

## 3. The journey (the scroll route)

Scrolling follows this route. Free flight is available at any time, with "back to route". The **astronaut** (our own, never Lusion's) is the guide who appears at the key moments.

| # | Moment | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Arrival** | The loading screen draws the triangle in engraved line art as the page loads. Seen from above, it's the logo. |
| 2 | **The rise** | Particles of light lift the triangle into the pyramid. The camera swings down to the opening shot from `inspiration/02`, and the astronaut drifts past toward it. |
| 3 | **Orbit** | A slow pass around the outside: the three corner landmarks, and the ad faces on the pyramid. |
| 4 | **The portal** | The astronaut flies through a ring of light in the three colours, then a short tunnel of glowing flower-of-life cells (from `inspiration/05`). We arrive inside, above the Arena. |
| 5 | **The Arena** | The battle floor, the round clock, the stream walls and the stadium tiers (section 4). |
| 6 | **Turn 1: the hubs** | The spiral's first turn. At each corner, a landing for that kind: **caller booths** (amber), **bot pods** (blue), **script screens** (violet). |
| 7 | **Turn 2: the Sector Ring** | Every entrant as a card in a ring orbiting the light column, ordered as listed or by name, **never by a stat**. Click a card to open their profile (section 6). |
| 8 | **Turn 3: the approach** | The spiral tightens; the walls carry the community's lights; kinetic words slide past ("CALLERS · BOTS · CHART SCRIPTS"). |
| 9 | **The Trade Floor** | The chamber at the peak, under a golden geometric crown (from `inspiration/04`). The community space. |
| 10 | **The bookend** | The astronaut returns beside "Apply for the next battle" and "Join the community". |

The **light column** in the centre is the fast lift: tap any level to rise or drop straight to it.

## 4. Places

> **Update 2026-10-17. This replaces the ramp described below.** The owner felt dizzy climbing the ramp and found stairs too literal. The proposal now:
> - **The Helix:** a **triple helix of entrants** rising up the centre from the Arena to the Trade Floor. Three strands twist together: an **amber strand of Callers**, a **blue strand of Bots** and a **violet strand of Chart Scripts**. Every entrant is a glowing card on its strand, ordered as listed or by name, never by a stat.
> - **Scrolling turns and raises the helix past a steady camera,** so the cards slide by one at a time. The camera never spins, which is what prevents dizziness.
> - **Filtering:** tap a kind and its strand glows and comes forward while the others dim. Tap a card to fly gently into that entrant's profile.
> - **Three calm levels:** the **Arena** (floor), the **Helix** (middle) and the **Trade Floor** (peak).
> - **The three inside walls** are each kind's gallery and stream wall: Callers amber, Bots blue, Chart Scripts violet. There are no ramps or railings.
> - **Comfort rules everywhere:**
>   - no continuous camera spin;
>   - a level horizon;
>   - vertical moves are slow lifts;
>   - reduce motion gets instant cuts.
>
> **Concept pictures:** `blueprint/helix-1.png` (outside), `helix-2.png` (inside, all strands), `helix-3.png` (filtered to Bots, with a card).
> **Fix for the build:** cards turn to face the viewer, so no text is ever seen mirrored from behind.
>
> **The grand battle hall (owner, 2026-10-17):** the battle takes place inside the pyramid **as a grand room**. Proposal, so the helix and the hall don't fight for the middle:
> - **The lower half of the pyramid is the Grand Hall:** an open floor for the battle, with the round clock as its inlaid centre, the stadium tiers around it and the stream walls on the three inner faces. Nothing stands in the middle.
> - **The Helix hangs from the peak like a great chandelier** over the upper half, ending above the hall. Visitors browse it by scrolling (it turns and rises), from a balcony level.
> - **When a battle starts,** the battle's entrants **peel off their strands and descend** into the hall, taking their stations around the clock in their colours. When it ends, they rise back up into the helix. This is the signature moment that joins the two.
> - **The Trade Floor stays at the peak,** above the helix.
>
> The ramp text below stays until the owner approves the change.

### Outside

- **The pyramid:** six edges of light, dark glass faces, particles flowing along the edges, and the crystal capstone.
- **The corner landmarks:** each base corner glows in its colour, with its emblem in line art: the Callers **spire** with broadcast rings, the Bots **crystal**, the Chart Scripts **dome**. They're landmarks seen from a distance; the real rooms are inside.
- **The faces:** the three outside faces are the world's main **billboards** (section 7).

### Inside

The ramp has three straight runs per turn, one along each inside face. The **landings at the corners are the kind hubs**, so every turn passes Callers, Bots and Scripts in their own colour, and the corners of the logo stack up the inside like floors of a tower.

- **The Arena (floor):** the battle core with its entrants orbiting and beaming in, and the green and red **round clock**. The **stadium tiers** rise around it, with the members online shown as small lights in the stands.
  - Above the Arena hang the **stream walls**: the three lower inner faces show the live streams of their colour's entrants. A **spotlight screen** enlarges one stream over the core; the viewer picks it, or it follows whoever just called.
- **Caller booths (amber hubs):** a glass booth per caller with their hologram silhouette and their verified stats (only what they've made public).
- **Bot pods (blue hubs):** a hexagon pod per bot, glowing while the bot is live in a battle.
- **Script screens (violet hubs):** floating chart panels per script, with lines and formulas drawn in light.
- **The Sector Ring (turn 2):** see section 3.
- **The Trade Floor (peak):** a glass floor looking down the column into the Arena, with wall screens for the community chat, the current round and live streams. The golden crown is overhead.

## 5. Live data: what comes from where

All of it is read from what the server already offers (`/api/v1` plus the live feed). Every privacy rule stays on the server: the world only draws what each reader is sent.

| In the world | Comes from |
| --- | --- |
| Entrants, hubs, the Sector Ring | `GET /api/v1/entrants` |
| Stats in booths, pods, screens and on profiles | Display slots (`world-callers`, `world-bots`, `world-scripts`, `world-arena`) and stat cards |
| The battle, lineup and board | `GET /api/v1/battles`, `/battles/:id/board`, the feed |
| The round clock | `GET /api/v1/rounds` |
| Calls arriving (sealed until the lock, then revealed) | The feed |
| Streams | The stream links kept in battle lineups |

## 6. Profiles

The cinematic style from `inspiration/06`:
- **Rich profiles** for Bots, Chart Scripts and Callers (to confirm: we read "read profiles" as callers):
  - full-screen hero art that changes as you scroll;
  - "I am **NAME.**" in the kind's colour, with HUD labels and the tagline in a glass card;
  - then verified stats, self-reported stats, battle history, recent calls (after their lock), their stream and trailer, and their Discord.
- **Simple profiles** for members: the same frame and type, with avatar, member-since date and activity.
- **Hero art:** generated engraved line art in the kind's colour by default. The owner's own upload, approved by HardSmoke, comes later as a server feature (we'll ask first).

## 7. Advertising: tasteful by design

The owner's rule: **never annoying.** So ads live **only on surfaces that belong to the world**, never on top of it.

**Where ads may appear:**

| Spot | How it behaves |
| --- | --- |
| **The three outside faces** (the main billboards) | Seen on arrival and in free flight. Only the face you're looking at plays. |
| **One billboard per spiral turn** (three inside in all) | Mid-way along a wall, between hubs, never on a hub or over a stream. |
| **The Arena intermission screen** | Only **between rounds**, after a round settles and before the next lock countdown. **Never during a lock or a reveal.** |
| **An entrant's own profile** | Their own trailer as a "Watch trailer" card. No other advertisers on someone's profile. |

**Never:**
- pop-ups, overlays or anything that covers the view;
- sound unless the visitor clicks;
- ads in the opening, the portal, on the Trade Floor (the community's space), or over live streams during a round;
- ads on sign-in, applications or any working page.

**Always:**
- every ad is labelled "Ad";
- muted, starting from a still image;
- several ads take turns per spot;
- on phones and Lite, it stays a still image until tapped;
- in 2D, at most one ad card per section.

**When there are no ads,** the spots show D3MON's own content: the next battle, "apply now", a featured entrant (as listed, never "top"), or our trailer. So spots are never empty, and the ad system can come last.

## 8. Navigation: clean, few buttons (owner, 2026-10-17)

The owner wants a **tidy site with far fewer buttons**, using grouped menus, and a **good, obvious place to apply for a battle**.

- **One slim top bar, three things only:**
  - the D3MON triangle logo (it opens the map: Arena, Callers, Bots, Chart Scripts, Trade Floor);
  - the **Apply for a battle** button;
  - the account menu.
- **One "Menu" drop-down holds everything else,** grouped:
  - **World:** the places;
  - **Battles:** the live board, the schedule, apply;
  - **Community:** chat, messages, coin;
  - **My D3MON:** profile, my bots and keys, settings;
  - **HardSmoke:** admin pages, shown to admins only.
- **"Apply for a battle"** is the only highlighted button.
  - It's always in the top bar, and also appears at the natural moments: beside the next battle in the Arena, on the astronaut's closing scene, and on empty entrant spots.
  - It changes to "Your application: pending / approved" once someone has applied.
- **The Quality switch and the sound toggle** sit as two small icons in a corner, not in the menu bar.
- **No toolbars inside the world:** places show their content, and actions appear only on hover or tap.
- **The same menu in 2D and on phones,** where it opens as a full-screen sheet.
- **All pages, not only the world:** the existing pages get the same tidy-up: the same top bar and menu, sections grouped, rarely used controls folded into menus or "More" panels. **Every existing control stays.** A control inventory per page is made first and checked off after each redesign.

## 9. Device levels

These are unchanged from the [plan](PLAN.md):
- **Full**, **Lite**, **Mobile Lite** and **2D**, starting at Lite and stepping up only once the device proves smooth.
- Reduce motion opens in 2D.
- The **2D version is a complete site**, not an error page: the same journey as a scrolling page, with the original background, the line-art emblems, every panel and live data. The 3D code is never downloaded there.

## 10. Build order

Each step is its own pull request, tested, with the server update walked through step by step.

1. **This blueprint:** approved by the owner.
2. **The `/world` foundation:** the page, device levels, the Quality switch, and the complete 2D version with the live panels.
3. **The 3D world, outside:** space, floor, the pyramid, the corners, the opening and the orbit, with the scroll route and free flight.
4. **The 3D world, inside:** the portal, the Arena (round clock, entrants, stream walls), the triangular spiral and the hubs.
5. **The Sector Ring, the Trade Floor and the astronaut.**
6. **Rich profiles,** using generated art first.
7. **The systems that need new server work, one at a time:** profile art uploads, then the **ad system** (spots, approval by HardSmoke, rotation, labels, simple counts). We'll ask about each first.
